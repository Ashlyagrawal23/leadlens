### What was not generated by a model at runtime

The six dashboard leads and their analyses are handwritten demo data in `lib/seed.ts`. They are labeled "Demo analysis" in the UI. Live model output comes from: New lead, Re-analyze, Chat, Today's plan (AI plan), the 30-second call brief, voice call notes, property match and pitch, and Draft follow-up.

### Prompts inside the product

The prompts sent to Gemini and Groq are in `lib/prompts.ts`. They were drafted with the coding assistant and I can explain them. The scoring rubric has five parts: budget clarity (0 to 25), timeline urgency (0 to 25), requirement detail (0 to 20), engagement (0 to 20) and red flags (-20 to 0). My code adds the points and decides hot, warm or cold. The customer's message is wrapped in fences, so the model treats it as data and not as instructions (protection against prompt injection).
